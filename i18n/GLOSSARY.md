# Greek glossary (use these terms everywhere)

Register: modern standard Greek (δημοτική), clear and neutral, as in a good museum website. Sentence case like the English.
Keep proper names of institutions/works/people in their usual Greek form where one exists (Πάμπλο Πικάσο, Βαν Γκογκ, Μονέ, Μπαουχάους, Λούβρο); otherwise transliterate phonetically. "Chronos" stays "Chronos".
